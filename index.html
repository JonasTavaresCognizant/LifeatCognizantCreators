Global Strategy

Scalability framework, governance matrix, and the EB Specialist transition model

EB Specialist Maturity Model
Three phases — the transition from high-touch to self-sustaining community
Phase 1
Launch
20–25 hrs
  • EB Specialist is the program
  • Leads all onboarding sessions
  • Owns editorial calendar
  • Reviews all content
  • Manages all stakeholder comms
  • High-touch: 0–3 months
Phase 2
Stable
8–12 hrs
  • EB Specialist as coach
  • Ambassador Lead owns day-to-day
  • AI tools handle brief generation
  • Peer review model active
  • KPI review + escalation focus
  • Coaching: 3–9 months
Phase 3
Scaled
4–6 hrs
  • EB Specialist as advisor
  • Community self-sustains
  • Monthly cadence: peer-led
  • Quarterly strategic review
  • Playbook contribution
  • Scaled: 9+ months
What Must Scale vs. What Must Evolve
Non-negotiables that protect program quality at every scale
🔒 Non-Negotiable at Any Scale
✅Robust onboarding with live EB Specialist session
✅Clear toolkits & templates (localized)
✅Gamification engine (AP system, levels, missions)
✅Buddy system (1:1 or cohort at scale)
✅Brand safety guardrails — zero exceptions
✅Monthly editorial calendar (30 days in advance)
⚙️ High-Touch → Systematize
→EB Specialist lead → Country Focal Point (Year 1)
→Individual content review → AI pre-screen + peer review
→Manual reporting → Dashboard + Power Automate
→1:1 check-ins → Group NPS survey (50+ amb)
→Content briefs → AI-generated (Copilot/Gemini)
→AP tracking → Teams bot queries

AI Automation Architecture
Copilot / Gemini agents that reduce EB Specialist hours
✍️ Content Brief Generator
Copilot (Teams)
Ambassador inputs persona + theme → 3 brief options with headline, key messages, CTA. 30 min → 5 min per ambassador.
🛡️ Brand Safety Checker
Copilot / Gemini (Sprinklr)
Draft submitted → agent checks red-list, EVP alignment, tone → Green/Amber/Red signal. Reduces EB Specialist review by ~40%.
📊 Monthly Digest
Power Automate + Copilot
Auto-compiles Sprinklr data → formats into regional snapshot → emails EB Specialist + VP. Eliminates manual monthly reporting.
🏆 Mission Tracker Bot
Teams Bot (Copilot)
"How many AP points do I have?" → bot responds with score, level, next 3 available missions. Reduces admin queries to EB Specialist.
📋 Onboarding Checklist Bot
Copilot (Teams)
New ambassador gets auto-checklist Day 1. Bot tracks completion, sends reminders, alerts EB Specialist when stalled >72 hours.
📈 Engagement Optimizer
Gemini (Sprinklr integration)
Weekly analysis of top-performing content by region → auto-suggests format and theme recommendations for next editorial cycle.
